IQR is the difference between the third quartile (Q3) and first quartile (Q1).
Formula
IQR=Q3−Q1
Outlier Detection Formula
Lower Limit:
Lower Limit=Q1−1.5×IQR
Upper Limit:
Upper Limit=Q3+1.5×IQR

Confusion Matrix is a table used to evaluate classification model performance.
It shows:
correct predictions
wrong predictions
Structure
[  TN  FP
  FN  TP  ]


Accuracy shows how many predictions are correct out of total predictions.
Formula
Accuracy=TP+TN/TP+TN+FP+FN

Error Rate shows how many predictions are wrong.
Formula
Error Rate=1−Accuracy

Precision tells how many predicted positives are actually correct.
Formula:
Precision= TP/TP+FP

Recall tells how many actual positives were correctly identified.
Formula
Recall=TP/TP+FN

TF measures how many times a word appears in a document.

Formula

TF=Number of times a term appears in a document/Total number of terms in the document

IDF measures how important a word is across all documents.
TF-IDF=TF×IDF
